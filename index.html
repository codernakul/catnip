General Instructions
  1. The clock is set at the server. The countdown timer at the top-right shows time remaining for the current section. When it reaches zero, the exam moves automatically to the next section.
  2. This exam enforces sectional time limits. You cannot go back to a previous section or extend its time once it has expired.
  3. The Question Palette on the right shows the status of every question in the current section. Click any numbered button to jump to that question.
  4. To save your answer, click "Save & Next". Simply selecting an option does not save it automatically.
  5. To flag a question for later review, click "Mark for Review & Next". If you have answered it, that answer will still be evaluated.
  6. To deselect your current answer, click "Clear Response".
  7. Click "Submit" only when you are finished with the entire exam. This action is irreversible.
Marking Scheme
  • MCQ questions: +3 marks for correct, −1 mark for incorrect, 0 for unattempted.
  • TITA questions: +3 marks for correct, no negative marking for an incorrect answer.
